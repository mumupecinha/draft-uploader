Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Draft Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner. It does not have other users and does not collect data from visitors to this site.

Data the app handles

  • A TikTok access token for the owner's own account, obtained through TikTok Login Kit and stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Video files and captions that the owner chooses to upload.

Sharing

This data is sent only to TikTok in order to create drafts. It is not sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the stored token at any time and can revoke the app's access in the TikTok app settings.
